# Portfolio — the glass-box table and the dive

Three working prototypes built on 2026-10-21 against `SPEC.md`, after Victor rejected the CSS zoom-and-door and the shrunken table. They are rough on purpose: they exist so he can pick a direction before the real implementation. The losing options stay here for reference, as the playbook asks.

| id | look | atom | door | push-in | file | artifact |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `vitrina` | museum glass case, dark room, top light, floor reflection | Bohr: warm nucleus, beads on tilted rings | front pane swings on its left hinge | through the pane into the nucleus, warm bloom | `vitrina.html` | https://claude.ai/artifact/JkP9ZaEgtCEk5drtXAmox7 |
| `holograma` | neon outlines on a black grid, faces appear on hover | shells as translucent spheres, electrons sliding on them | front face dissolves into a particle curtain | through each shell, blue-white bloom | `holograma.html` | https://claude.ai/artifact/E7iQtK8UpuWjGztpsnUCCj |
| `caja` | physical specimen boxes, glass front, engraved plate, warm gallery light | packed nucleus cluster, satellites on rings | glass lid tilts open from the top | into the parting nucleus, amber bloom | `caja.html` | https://claude.ai/artifact/H4chyw5bGi398noUgDSDhH |

Shared in all three: the table owns the viewport, the side card by column, the dive that keeps the neighbors visible, Volver reverses everything, `?simple=1` for weak GPUs, real electron counts from `demo-data.js` (generated by `tools/portfolio-data.mjs`).

How to look at one locally: open the `.html` file in a browser (it loads three.js from cdnjs and the hero photographs from Commons; everything else is inline). The `.artifact.html` copies are the same pages without the document wrapper, for publishing as claude.ai artifacts.

What happens after the pick: the chosen option replaces the CSS tile grid in `src/table/` with a three.js scene; the lenses paint the boxes (family glow, state, spectra as light inside the box); the CSS table remains as the simple mode; the dive lands on the deck exactly as today.

Caja's dive timeline was switched to the wall clock after the review (a throttled frame loop made it crawl).
